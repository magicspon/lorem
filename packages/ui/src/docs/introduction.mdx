# @repo/ui

The component library behind tuesday: [Base UI](https://base-ui.com) components styled with
[StyleX](https://stylexjs.com). Every look comes from a small set of **tokens**, and every
component composes them. The app writes no styles of its own.

## How it fits together

1. **Tokens** live in `styles/tokens.stylex.ts`: `colors`, `space`, `size`, `fontSize`,
   `lineHeight`, `radii`, `fonts` and `motion`, with `shadows` beside them. A component reads a
   token, never a literal colour or length. The lint rules `no-raw-length` and `no-raw-shadow`
   check this.
2. **Components** live in one folder each, `src/<group>/<name>/`, and are imported by that path:
   `@repo/ui/actions/button`. A component with several parts is a namespace (`Card.Root`,
   `Card.Header`).
3. **Looks are variants, not props.** A component offers a few named variants
   (`<Button variant="outline">`) rather than width, gap or colour props. A look that is missing
   becomes a new variant here.
4. **`sx` composes.** Every part takes `sx`, merged after its own styles. To give another element
   a component's look, pass its exported styles: `<Link sx={buttonStyles()} />`.
5. **Layout primitives** (`Box`, `Flex`, `Stack`, `Grid`, `Container`, `Section`) take tokens
   only, with a value per breakpoint where it helps: `gap={{ initial: '2', md: '4' }}`.

## Colour

Colours come from [Radix Colors](https://www.radix-ui.com/colors). Each scale has 12 steps, and
each step is designed for one use:

<StepUses />

The tokens keep semantic names (`primary`, `border`, `mutedForeground`) and point each one at the
step that fits its use. Sage is the neutral, mint is the primary, red is destructive, green is
success and amber is warning. The `A` scales are the same steps with transparency, for fills that
sit over any surface.

<ColorScales />

`scripts/radix-colors.mjs` copies the scales from `@radix-ui/colors` into
`styles/radix.stylex.ts`, because StyleX reads only values it can see when it compiles. To use
another scale, add it to the script and run `pnpm --filter @repo/ui colors`.

### Light and dark

Every colour token holds both schemes in `light-dark()`, so the page follows the system's
setting with nothing to switch. To choose a scheme over the system's, apply `lightTheme` or
`darkTheme` from `@repo/ui/styles/themes` to any element. `ColorSchemeToggle` switches to the
other scheme with one press, and back to the system's with the next.

## Typography

Text uses `Text` and `Heading` from `typography/`, on a nine-step scale. A heading's `level`
picks the element, and its `size` picks the look, so the outline stays correct whatever the
size. Inter is the typeface.

## Space

`space` is for gap, padding and margin only, in nine steps. A layout prop takes the step, not a
length: `<Stack gap="3" p="4">`. Other fixed lengths, such as control heights and icon sizes, come
from `size`, keyed by multiples of 0.25rem (`size['9']` is 2.25rem).

<SpaceScale />

## Shadows

Every box shadow is a token in `styles/shadows.stylex.ts`; a component never writes one. They
take their colours from the Radix steps: focus rings on step 8, the hairline round a popup or
menu on step 6, and elevation from `colors.shadow`, step 9. In dark the elevation shadows use
Radix's `blackA` scale, since a dark alpha step would lighten. A shadow barely shows on a dark
page, so every surface that floats also draws a hairline: popups, menus and tooltips in the
shadow itself, and `raised` and `floating` with the border they are always paired with.

## Interactive states

Every control sets its hover, pressed, focus and disabled looks from the same tokens: the hover
and pressed fills one Radix step apart, the `ring` border and `focusRing` halo on focus, and half
opacity when disabled. A theme changes them once for the whole library.
